Rules

  • Roll a 6 and you go again.
  • Land on a ladder foot, climb it.
  • Land on a snake head, slide down.
  • You need an exact roll to land on 100. Overshoot and you stay put.
  • 🐍 Moving snakes mode: snakes crawl back and forth along the whole board in real time, each at its own random speed. If one's body is currently over your square, it bites you — even between turns. Ladders stay put.
  • Rare green snakes are friendly — they'll boost you forward instead of biting.
  • 🦘 Each player gets 3 jumps per game: hop 3 squares ahead instantly, no dice needed — handy for dodging an incoming snake.
  • Past 16 snakes, panic mode kicks in: the board glows red and everything speeds up.
